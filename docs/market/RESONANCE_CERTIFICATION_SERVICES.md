## Market claim boundary

RCS is an internal Resonance certification standard. It must not be marketed as ISO certification, accreditation, regulatory approval, legal advice, or third-party conformity certification.

External standards may be used as assessment criteria through the approved applicability profile. A standards-alignment report is not itself an external certification.

## Issuance boundary

No certificate is issued merely because a service was purchased, requested, or completed. Issuance requires:

1. defined assessment scope;
2. approved standards/applicability profile;
3. captured traceable evidence;
4. resolved blocking findings;
5. closed assessment;
6. authorized human certification decision;
7. explicit validity boundary.

Automation may prepare evidence and records but cannot self-issue certification.

## Commercial boundary

The current offering is intentionally priced as a published introductory business offering. Billing remains disabled. Future pricing requires a separate governed commercial decision and does not alter certification criteria.

## Transparency

A market-facing certificate claim should expose the certification class, scope, RCS version, state, issue/expiry or review dates, evidence reference where publishable, and material limitations.
