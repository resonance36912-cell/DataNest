## 1. Pledge

We publicly declare our intent to build, operate and improve DataNest and the wider Resonance AppDev ecosystem as a transparent, evidence-led, human-governed system.

We pledge that useful capability will not be pursued by silently transferring authority from humans to AI, by weakening security or privacy boundaries, by converting provisional evidence into unsupported assurance, or by representing a private system as a statutory regulator, accredited certification body, registered audit firm or other reserved authority without the required independent evidence and authorization.

This declaration is a **pledge of intent and governance direction**. It becomes an adopted governance instrument only after the required human approval has been recorded.

## 2. Scope

Our scope covers the governed lifecycle of Resonance application-development projects, products, services, packages and supporting infrastructure, including:

- source and provenance;
- product and service intent;
- AI-assisted planning and development;
- testing, validation and release evidence;
- certification/readiness and assurance evidence;
- security, privacy and environment controls;
- operations, maintenance and recovery;
- governance and regulatory-readiness processes;
- transparency and audit evidence;
- user/stakeholder value and route-to-market modelling;
- sovereign replication and registry development.

The canonical DataNest System Charter remains the controlling scope reference.

## 3. Mission

Our mission is to provide a transparent, evidence-led human-AI operating system for building and improving Resonance digital products and services with lower friction, stronger quality, safer automation, resilient infrastructure and continuously improving user and stakeholder value.

## 4. Vision

We intend to establish a sovereign, interoperable AppDev environment in which:

1. intent progresses through an explicit evidence chain;
2. humans retain final authority for consequential decisions;
3. AI continuously assists quality, usability, health, security and market-fit work without obscuring provenance;
4. infrastructure remains replaceable where practical;
5. stakeholders can inspect claims, evidence and governance boundaries;
6. route-to-market decisions remain reviewable and evidence-labelled;
7. learning compounds without automatically becoming unquestionable institutional truth.

## 5. Governance commitment

We will preserve the following authority sequence:

```
intent
→ evidence / design
→ R&D or implementation
→ verification
→ security / environment / boundaries assessment
→ optimization and audit evidence
→ human review where consequential
→ explicit production authorization
→ exact-version deployment
→ monitoring / learning / maintenance
```

AI systems and specialized trees may observe, test, model, recommend, coordinate and perform narrowly bounded reversible maintenance. They do not self-authorize production, financial commitments, legal representations, governance changes, credential acquisition or weakening of mandatory controls.

The hard governance floor remains:

- production-authority separation;
- Boundaries;
- ENFORCER blockers;
- secret protection;
- unique-history protection;
- mandatory human approval;
- reproducible verification for the affected change class.

## 6. Service and product delivery

We intend to deliver products and services through explicit scopes, authorization records, evidence registers, assumptions, exclusions, findings, remediation plans and reviewable outcomes.

Our independent assurance and audit-support offering remains expressly bounded. It may provide regulatory-readiness, technical-audit support, evidence, remediation, control verification and authorized digital-oversight services. It does not by itself confer statutory regulator status, IRBA registration, accredited certification-body status, guaranteed compliance or authority to conduct unauthorized surveillance.

Reserved or regulated activities will be handled only within the applicable lawful registration/accreditation/authorization boundary or through an appropriately qualified and authorized party.

## 7. REGULATOR and operational process

The REGULATOR tree is a transparency and harmony mechanism. It may convert unresolved conditions into requirements, audit and stress-test, identify lawful free/open/already-authorized resources, assess readiness and prepare bounded action plans.

It may not purchase, commit funds, acquire credentials, change Boundaries, bypass security, merge or deploy production.

Other specialized trees retain their documented boundaries, including Knowledge, Boundaries, BOTSQUAD, ENVIRONMENT, ENFORCER, GUARDIAN, CONDUCTOR, SUGGESTER, CALMER, VISIBILITY-UTILITY, Mirror-DataNest and FREETREE.

## 8. Universal-law and RSGP commitment

We commit to operate consistently with applicable law, lawful authority, human rights and fundamental governance principles, and with the project's adopted RSGP requirements.

Where a legal, regulatory or standards proposition requires jurisdiction-specific interpretation, registration, accreditation, certification, statutory authority or professional standing, we will distinguish:

**intent → alignment → evidence → independent verification → formal status.**

We will not collapse these states into one another.

A standards mapping is not represented as certification. A readiness assessment is not represented as statutory approval. An AI-generated recommendation is not represented as human authorization.

## 9. Pull-and-push evidence protocol

Evidence is to move in both directions without losing provenance:

**PULL**
- retrieve source documents, repository state, control definitions, implementation evidence, test results, audit findings and relevant external evidence;
- record source, date/time, scope, version and limitations;
- preserve the distinction between observation, interpretation, recommendation and authorization.

**PUSH**
- publish appropriate sanitized evidence into the canonical repository;
- link evidence to the affected declaration, issue, PR, release, service record or audit package;
- preserve hashes, commit identities, reviewer records and decision history where available;
- expose evidence gaps rather than filling them with unsupported claims.

Public transparency excludes secrets, private authentication material and protected personal information.

## 10. External AI auditor call

We formally invite an **independent external AI auditor / AI assurance reviewer** to review this declaration and its evidence chain.

The external reviewer is requested to examine, at minimum:

1. scope, mission and vision;
2. authority separation and human-approval gates;
3. AI role boundaries and non-self-authorization;
4. evidence provenance and pull/push traceability;
5. regulatory and certification-claim boundaries;
6. RSGP alignment as evidenced by the project record;
7. service/product delivery controls;
8. REGULATOR and specialized-tree boundaries;
9. security, privacy and transparency controls;
10. release and production-authority separation.

The reviewer should publish or return:

- reviewer identity;
- organization or independent capacity;
- independence/conflict-of-interest attestation;
- exact scope reviewed;
- evidence/reference set;
- methods or limitations;
- findings and unresolved risks;
- conclusion stated as accepted / needs changes / rejected, or another clearly defined assurance disposition;
- date and version reviewed;
- cryptographic or repository reference where practical.

Opening or receiving this review does **not** itself constitute approval.

## 11. Human approval gate

This declaration is intentionally published as **PROPOSED / HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED**.

Human approval must be explicit and attributable. Until that approval is recorded:

- this document is not a final governance authorization;
- it does not authorize production deployment;
- it does not authorize financial commitments;
- it does not establish statutory or professional status;
- it does not override existing repository protections or required review gates.

After human approval, the adopted version must retain its approval evidence and any material amendments must be reviewable.

## 13. Amendment and challenge

Any person or organization with relevant evidence may challenge a claim, identify an evidence gap, request clarification or propose an amendment.

Challenges should preserve the distinction between:

- fact and interpretation;
- evidence and assertion;
- recommendation and authorization;
- readiness and certification;
- internal governance and statutory authority.

We commit to correcting materially unsupported public claims when credible evidence demonstrates that they are unsupported.

## 14. Pledge statement

**We choose evidence before authority, human approval before consequential action, transparency without unsafe disclosure, and continuous improvement without surrendering human agency.**

**We invite independent scrutiny.**

**We will treat external AI audit as an assurance input, not as a substitute for human accountability.**

**Human approval remains the final gate for adoption of this declaration.**
