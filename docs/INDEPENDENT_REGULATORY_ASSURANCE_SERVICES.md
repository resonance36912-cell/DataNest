# DataNest Independent Regulatory Assurance & Audit Services

**Status:** Commercial service catalogue — private independent service, not a statutory regulator.

## Market position

DataNest offers evidence-first governance, technical audit support, compliance-readiness assessment, authorized digital monitoring, control verification, implementation planning, and continuous oversight for software, AI, data, cloud, and digital-operating environments.

DataNest does **not** represent itself as a South African government regulator, an accredited certification body, or an IRBA-registered audit firm. Where a client requires a statutory audit opinion, regulated assurance engagement, accredited certification, or private-security service, DataNest will scope the work as readiness/support or require an appropriately registered/accredited partner before accepting the reserved activity.

## Authorized monitoring / surveillance boundary

DataNest's monitoring service is limited to systems, repositories, logs, workflows, public sources, assets and environments that the client owns or is authorized to monitor.

The service excludes, unless separately lawful and appropriately registered:
- covert interception;
- unlawful employee/person tracking;
- physical guarding or private-security surveillance;
- access to third-party systems without authorization;
- credential bypass;
- spyware;
- collection that is unnecessary for the contracted purpose.

Where DataNest processes personal information on behalf of a client, the engagement must define the responsible-party/operator relationship, confidentiality, security safeguards, access controls, retention and breach-escalation responsibilities.

## South African regulatory boundary

- **IRBA:** statutory audit and regulated assurance work must be handled by appropriately registered auditors/firms where the Auditing Profession Act and IRBA rules apply.
- **PSiRA:** security services for remuneration may require PSiRA registration; DataNest therefore does not market physical/private-security services as part of this offer.
- **POPIA/PAIA:** monitoring and evidence processing involving personal information must follow lawful-processing, minimisation, confidentiality, security-safeguard and incident-reporting requirements.
- **CIPC:** company/business registration does not itself confer statutory-regulator status.

## Engagement flow

1. Client submits a Trade & Implementation Interest request.
2. DataNest performs a no-cost fit/scope screen.
3. Written scope, authorization, deliverables, exclusions and fee are agreed.
4. Assessment/monitoring executes under the approved scope.
5. Findings and evidence are issued.
6. Remediation and implementation are offered separately or under the relevant plan.
7. Reserved regulated work is referred to or delivered with an appropriately registered/accredited professional where required.
