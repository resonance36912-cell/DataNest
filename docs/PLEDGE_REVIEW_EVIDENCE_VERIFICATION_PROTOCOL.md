# Pledge Review Evidence & Verification Protocol

**Instrument:** Pledge of Oath & Tender in Honesty and Transparency in Free-Will v1.0  
**Status:** Proposed / Human Approval Required  
**Review issue:** #393  
**Implementation PR:** #392  
**Repository baseline:** commit `b132e1df2a6360f1baccf813212c55a95012a1b4`

## 1. Purpose

This protocol gives reviewers a reproducible method for examining the proposed Pledge and reporting findings without conflating proposal, evidence, review, approval, or adoption.

## 2. Canonical artefact

The canonical proposed instrument is:

`docs/PLEDGE_OF_OATH_HONESTY_TRANSPARENCY_FREE_WILL.md`

Reviewers should record the exact commit, branch/ref, or repository state examined.

## 3. Evidence states

For every material proposition, reviewers should classify the record as one or more of:

- **PLEDGED:** an explicit voluntary commitment stated by the instrument.
- **CLAIMED:** a statement made by a participant or organization.
- **EVIDENCED:** supported by an identifiable record or reproducible evidence.
- **INDEPENDENTLY REVIEWED:** examined by a reviewer whose independence and scope are stated.
- **APPROVED:** explicitly authorized by the competent human authority.
- **ADOPTED:** entered into the applicable governance baseline.

A later state must not be inferred merely because an earlier state exists.

## 4. Reviewer procedure

1. Obtain the canonical repository state and record the commit/ref.
2. Read the complete Pledge, including governance notices and limitations.
3. Identify each material claim or control relevant to the review scope.
4. Request or inspect supporting evidence where a claim requires substantiation.
5. Record scope, assumptions, exclusions, limitations, conflicts, and unresolved questions.
6. Distinguish factual observations from interpretation or recommendation.
7. Report findings with severity or materiality criteria defined by the reviewer.
8. Identify evidence sufficient to close each material finding.
9. Preserve a traceable record of the reviewed version and any subsequent amendments.

## 5. Independence and conflicts

A reviewer should state any actual, potential, or reasonably perceived conflict of interest that could materially affect the review. The existence of a reviewer response does not itself establish independence, competence, accreditation, authorization, or certification.

## 6. Minimum finding record

Where practical, each finding should contain:

- finding identifier;
- reviewed artefact and exact version;
- observation;
- evidence/reference;
- requirement or criterion used;
- impact/risk statement;
- uncertainty or limitation;
- requested clarification or corrective action;
- reviewer disposition/status.

## 7. Governance boundaries

This protocol does not authorize production deployment, legal representation, financial activity, certification, accreditation, regulatory approval, or any other authority outside the applicable governance process.

External review is an assurance input. Human approval remains the final adoption gate.

## 8. Publication rule

Review outputs should preserve enough context for another party to determine:

**what was reviewed → when it was reviewed → against what criteria → from what evidence → by whom/under what declared independence → what was found → what remains unresolved.**

Convenience copies may be distributed for review, but the repository state and cited commit remain the controlling provenance reference.

## 9. Review tender

All good-faith findings, including objections, contradictions, evidence failures, ambiguity, and proposed amendments, are within scope. No endorsement is required for participation.
