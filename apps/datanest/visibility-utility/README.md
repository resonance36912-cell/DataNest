# VISIBILITY-UTILITY Tree

**VISIBILITY-UTILITY** is DataNest's continuous discoverability, market-presence and route-to-market intelligence tree.

It audits technical SEO, public metadata, brand/platform coverage, product/service discoverability, public evidence and acquisition readiness. It can ingest configured global/local trend, analytics and commercial feeds. When those feeds are unavailable it records the missing evidence rather than inventing market data.

The tree produces ranked route-to-market scenarios with explicit assumptions, relative reach/activation projections and confidence. Revenue/sales-value projections are produced only when a real commercial baseline is supplied.

Generated metadata and evidence may refresh automatically. Campaign launches, paid acquisition, pricing, sales commitments, public claims, provider/platform changes and user-facing functions remain review-required through SUGGESTER/CONDUCTOR and the existing DataNest governance path.

## Current commercial route-to-market asset

The independent regulatory-assurance offer is published at `/DataNest/assurance/` with search metadata, transparent pricing, a public service charter and a dedicated Trade & Implementation Interest intake form.

Market claims are intentionally bounded: DataNest markets independent assessment, technical-audit support, regulatory-readiness, evidence, remediation and authorized digital oversight—not statutory regulator, certification-body or registered-audit-firm status.
