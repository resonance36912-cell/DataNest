# Pledge of Oath & Tender in Honesty and Transparency in Free-Will

**Status:** Proposed Governance Instrument — Open for independent review and human consideration  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Governance alignment:** RSGP Governance  
**Repository:** DataNest  
**Date:** 2026-10-01

## 1. Purpose

This Pledge establishes a voluntary commitment to act, communicate, govern, review, and deliver in honesty and transparency, while preserving free will, human agency, due process, and the right to informed consent.

It is a governance commitment, not a statutory oath, legal certification, accreditation, regulatory designation, or substitute for applicable law.

## 2. The Pledge

I freely and knowingly pledge, to the best of my knowledge, capacity, and lawful authority:

1. **Honesty** — I will seek to represent facts, evidence, intentions, limitations, uncertainty, and material changes truthfully and without deliberate deception.
2. **Transparency** — I will make material information reasonably available for appropriate scrutiny, subject to lawful privacy, confidentiality, security, and legitimate safety constraints.
3. **Free-Will** — I will respect the capacity of people to make informed and voluntary decisions. I will not represent consent as having been given where it has not been given.
4. **Human Agency** — I will not transfer personal accountability to an automated system, algorithm, institution, or delegated process merely because it was involved in a decision.
5. **Evidence Before Authority** — Where a claim, decision, certification, assurance, or governance action depends upon evidence, I will seek to establish and preserve an auditable evidence trail before treating the matter as established.
6. **Disclosure of Uncertainty** — Where facts are incomplete, disputed, inferred, estimated, or not independently verified, I will identify that condition rather than present it as established fact.
7. **Correction** — Where I discover a material error, omission, misleading statement, or unsupported assertion attributable to my actions, I will seek timely correction and preserve the relevant record of the correction.
8. **Conflict Disclosure** — I will disclose material conflicts of interest, incentives, relationships, or dependencies where their omission could reasonably affect trust, interpretation, or decision-making.
9. **Non-Retaliation for Good-Faith Scrutiny** — I will support good-faith questioning, challenge, audit, verification, and reporting of material concerns without retaliation for the act of raising them.
10. **Independent Review** — I will permit appropriate independent human and technical review of claims and governance artefacts, recognizing that an auditor's independence and conclusions must not be manufactured, suppressed, or predetermined.
11. **Traceability** — Where practicable, material governance actions will retain provenance sufficient to identify what was proposed, what evidence supported it, who or what performed each action, what was approved, and what remains unresolved.
12. **Lawful Boundaries** — This Pledge does not authorize conduct that is unlawful, does not override contractual or statutory duties, and does not create authority that does not otherwise exist.
13. **Voluntary Commitment** — Participation in this Pledge is by free and informed choice. A person may decline, withdraw, or request clarification without that choice being falsely represented as consent.
14. **No False Assurance** — Neither this Pledge nor its publication shall be represented as proof that a person, system, organization, product, service, or process is honest, compliant, safe, certified, accredited, or lawful. Such claims require their own evidence and applicable authorization.
15. **Continuous Accountability** — The Pledge is an ongoing commitment to responsible conduct, review, correction, and learning rather than a one-time declaration that permanently settles future conduct.

## 3. Tender of the Pledge

The Pledge is tendered openly for consideration by individuals, contributors, maintainers, reviewers, auditors, service providers, governance participants, and other interested parties.

A person or organization adopting it should record, according to the applicable governance process:

- the identity or accountable role of the pledging party;
- the date and version accepted;
- the scope of the commitment;
- any declared limitations or reservations;
- the evidence or governance record supporting adoption; and
- any subsequent withdrawal, amendment, suspension, or correction.

No person's silence, participation in a discussion, use of a system, or receipt of information should be represented as acceptance of the Pledge.

## 4. RSGP Governance Alignment

This instrument is intended to operate within the governance principles and controls applicable to RSGP Governance, including:

- human approval and accountability for consequential governance decisions;
- separation between proposal, evidence, independent review, approval, adoption, and publication;
- provenance and traceability of material evidence;
- explicit scope, limitations, and jurisdictional boundaries;
- external scrutiny and the opportunity for good-faith challenge; and
- preservation of free will and informed human decision-making.

This document states alignment as a governance objective. It does not by itself establish certification or conformance to any external standard.

## 5. Review and Adoption Gate

This Pledge is published as a **proposed governance instrument**.

Its publication does not constitute final adoption. Adoption should follow the applicable governance path, including independent review where required and explicit human authorization by the competent authority.

No audit result, approval, certification, accreditation, regulatory status, or adoption decision may be implied before it actually occurs.

## 6. Auditability

Reviewers should be able to distinguish, at minimum:

- **pledged:** what the party voluntarily commits to do;
- **claimed:** what the party says has occurred;
- **evidenced:** what records substantiate;
- **independently reviewed:** what an appropriately independent reviewer has examined;
- **approved:** what the competent human authority has expressly authorized; and
- **adopted:** what has actually entered the applicable governance baseline.

These states must not be collapsed into one another.

## 7. Good-Faith Tender

The Pledge is tendered in good faith for discussion, scrutiny, amendment, and—where duly approved—implementation. Criticism and proposed improvement are part of transparent governance and should be preserved as part of the review record where material.
