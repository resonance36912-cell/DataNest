## 1. Executive statement

**Resonance Sole Proprietorship** is the business. **Resonance AppDev** is the master brand. **DataNest** is the canonical operating/control platform beneath that brand for planning, developing, testing, validating, promoting, operating, learning from and transparently improving products and services. Its market-facing portfolio is broader than DataNest itself: **Resonance** is the customer application suite, **Sovereign Forge** is a sibling professional/B2B line, and **Resonance AppDev Assurance Services** is a professional service line.

The system is designed around a simple operating principle:

> **Increase useful capability and business value while preserving human agency, evidence, security, reversibility, transparency and explicit authority.**

DataNest combines human decision-making with bounded AI assistance. AI trees may observe, learn, recommend, coordinate, test, model and perform narrowly defined reversible maintenance. They do not silently acquire production authority, financial authority, legal authority, governance authority or the right to weaken non-negotiable controls.

DataNest uses GitHub as canonical source/history/CI/evidence authority, GitHub Pages as the current public delivery target, Supabase as the governed application/backend authority, and Dropbox as the governed backup-artifact host. Reson8 Forge is a sovereign replication/registry/automation extension and does not silently replace canonical authority.

DataNest aims to become a sovereign, interoperable product operating environment in which:

1. products and services move from intent to validated delivery through an explicit evidence chain;
2. humans retain final authority for consequential decisions;

## 6. Operating principles

DataNest applies these principles across all operating trees and services under the Resonance AppDev master brand:

1. **Human agency** — consequential decisions remain reviewable and reversible.
2. **Evidence before authority** — evidence can support a decision but does not itself become authorization.
3. **Least authority** — every human, workflow and AI tree receives only the authority it requires.
4. **Traceability** — important outputs retain source, time, identity and evidence lineage.
5. **Fail closed on uncertainty** — UNKNOWN is not treated as permission.
6. **Reversibility** — autonomous change is restricted to reversible or provably redundant cases.
7. **Security and privacy by design** — secrets, authentication material and protected data are not transparency content.
8. **Transparency without unsafe disclosure** — public accountability does not require publishing credentials or private information.
9. **Provider replaceability** — infrastructure is not identity or governance authority.
10. **Continuous improvement** — health, quality, UX, market fit and governance are measured and iterated.
11. **Accessible human-centred design** — usability and accessibility are design requirements, not decorative extras.
12. **No false assurance** — certification, compliance, market and financial claims must match evidence.

| Source, history, CI and evidence | GitHub · DataNest-Supository/DataNest |
| Public delivery | GitHub Pages |

| Sovereign Forge extension | Reson8 Forge in governed-mirror mode |
| R&D candidate environment | Mirror-DataNest |
| Isolated open-development environment | FREETREE |

### 7.2 Governance sequence

A material production change follows the principle:

```text
intent
  → evidence / design
  → R&D or implementation
  → verification
  → security / environment / boundaries assessment
  → optimization and audit evidence
  → human review where consequential
  → explicit production authorization
  → exact-version deployment
  → monitoring / learning / maintenance
```

### 7.3 Hard governance floor

No optimization tree may remove:

- production authorization separation;
- hard Boundaries;
- ENFORCER blockers;
- secret protection;
- unique-history protection;
- mandatory human approval;
- reproducible verification required for the affected change class.

CALMER may minimize unnecessary planning/review friction **above** that floor, but cannot lower the floor itself.

## 8. Specialized-tree architecture

### 8.1 Knowledge

Learns from canonical and Mirror repository evidence, preserves provenance, categorizes patterns, and generates provisional optimization feeds. Knowledge does not self-promote observations into certified institutional truth.

### 8.2 Boundaries

Defines tolerance limits, acceptance criteria and machine-verifiable invariants. It is the canonical expression of DataNest human-AI governance constraints.

### 8.3 BOTSQUAD

Provides scoped AI-only advisory branches for optimization, product expansion, business growth, UX, UI, function evolution, integration, quality, environment and regulatory tasks. BOTSQUAD recommendations do not authorize production.

### 8.4 ENVIRONMENT

Assesses the shared runtime, CI, delivery, backend, inference, registry, backup, dependency, branch and evidence environment required by the other trees.

### 8.5 ENFORCER

Monitors security and defensive state against Boundaries. It consumes approved Knowledge without topic/category suppression while keeping secrets and protected data outside public transparency.

### 8.6 GUARDIAN

Takes health snapshots, compares observed state with an optimal blueprint and previous snapshots, detects drift, and may heal only proven redundant branches and byte-safe source noise. Behavioral refactoring remains reviewed.

### 8.7 CONDUCTOR

Coordinates freshness, dependency order, pull/push and push/pull timing. It dispatches at most one coordinated process per cycle and fails closed on stale, failed or active prerequisites.

### 8.8 SUGGESTER

Continuously synthesizes health, learning, security, environment, AI advisory and coordination evidence into deduplicated optimization suggestions. Its autonomous command queue is limited to pre-approved reversible maintenance actions.

### 8.9 CALMER

Minimizes avoidable governance friction while preserving the hard pass floor. It packages functions, processes and builds into:

- **STONE** — baseline passing evidence and minimum-friction routing;
- **GOLD** — stronger accuracy/performance/security/contribution evidence and accelerated low-risk routing;
- **DIAMOND** — maximum permitted non-authorizing fast-lane treatment, including the configured full-security threshold.

These tiers are workflow incentives and evidence packages, not permissions, certifications, ownership, financial rights or governance votes.

### 8.10 REGULATOR

Converts unresolved system conditions into transparent requirements and uses dedicated BOTSQUAD roles to audit, stress-test, identify free/open/already-authorized resource options, assess supply readiness and prepare bounded action plans.

REGULATOR cannot purchase, commit funds, acquire credentials, change Boundaries, bypass security, merge or deploy production.

### 8.11 VISIBILITY-UTILITY

Continuously assesses:

- technical SEO;
- search discoverability;
- public evidence;
- brand consistency;
- platform/application coverage;
- user acquisition readiness;
- stakeholder acquisition readiness;
- resource/service discoverability;
- sales readiness;
- configured global and local trends;
- route-to-market scenarios.

It produces evidence-labelled market scenarios and projected relative outcomes for consideration. It does not guarantee outcomes and does not authorize advertising spend, pricing, sales commitments or unverified public claims.

### 8.12 Mirror-DataNest

Ungated/rapid R&D and production-parity candidate environment. Mirror may test and produce candidate evidence, but it cannot silently replace canonical DataNest production authority.

### 8.13 FREETREE

Isolated open-development tree/repository. It remains outside automatic DataNest/Mirror synchronization and does not obtain canonical production authority.

## 22. Metrics and operating targets

The following are **management targets**, not current-state claims or contractual guarantees.

### 22.1 Reliability and health

- GUARDIAN health target: ≥ 90/100;
- zero unresolved critical security blockers before governed production authorization;
- zero autonomous deletion of unique history;
- deterministic recovery evidence for material continuity changes.

### 22.2 Quality

- all required CI and verification gates passing for governed production changes;
- high-severity Workflow Reviewer findings target: 0 at release;
- reproducible product/test evidence tied to exact build identity.

### 22.3 Governance

- 100% of consequential production changes linked to explicit authority;
- 100% of autonomous mutations restricted to approved reversible classes;
- zero hidden production-authority transfer.

### 22.4 Accessibility and UX

- WCAG 2.2-oriented verification for public/product surfaces;
- no known blocker preventing keyboard completion of primary journeys;
- visible, understandable failure and permission state.

### 22.5 Visibility and market presence

- technical SEO score target: ≥ 90;
- public catalog delivery coverage target: ≥ 95% for intentionally public products/services;
- public charter, audit and application metadata discoverable;
- global/local trend inputs explicitly configured for markets where decisions depend on them;
- route-to-market projections tagged with evidence coverage and confidence.

### 22.6 CALMER efficiency

- reduce duplicated planning/evidence friction while maintaining hard controls;
- measure baseline vs softened review-planning windows;
- suspend softening when security or critical health conditions are not met.

```text
  → retain learning
```

A closed item should retain evidence of what changed and why closure is justified.

## 27. Market and commercial governance

Market growth is a legitimate optimization objective, but commercial action must remain attributable.

VISIBILITY-UTILITY and BOTSQUAD may:

- identify opportunities;
- model channels;
- propose campaigns;
- identify resources;
- recommend service positioning;
- estimate relative outcomes;
- propose sales-enablement improvements.

They may not autonomously:

- spend money;
- sign contracts;
- change pricing;
- make warranties or guarantees;
- claim certification;
- misrepresent market data;
- make binding offers.

## 28. Data and privacy

Data use should follow purpose limitation, least privilege, retention discipline and evidence minimization.

Where analytics or market feeds are connected:

- identify the data source and purpose;
- minimize personal data;
- avoid exporting private user content into public feeds;
- document retention;
- apply access controls;
- preserve lawful/contractual obligations applicable to the operating jurisdiction.

This charter is an architecture and management-system statement, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

## 32. LIBERTY-IN-ALL continuous traceability standard

DataNest adopts **LIBERTY-IN-ALL** as its internal continuous indexing and traceability standard: maximum legitimate visibility with minimum necessary restriction. The standard hashes attributable records, binds indexes to exact Git source refs, exposes missing evidence, and publishes sanitized metadata for interested individuals, stakeholders, auditors and regulators.

LIBERTY-IN-ALL does **not** publish arbitrary record contents, secrets, private authentication material or protected personal data. Disclosure, retention and sensitivity-policy changes remain human-reviewed. The standard cannot approve, merge, deploy production, rewrite historical evidence or change production authority.

Its standards mapping includes ISO 15489-1:2016, ISO 23081-1:2017 and W3C PROV, together with the charter's existing information-security, privacy and governance mappings. These are alignment references only and do not constitute certification or a legal-compliance determination.
