Transparency boundary

  • Audit documents are informational evidence and do not grant governance, financial, ownership or role authority.
  • External findings will be identified with stable AUD-xxx IDs and distinguished from the audit methodology.
  • Remediation evidence should link findings to governed work, source changes, migrations, tests, releases and post-release verification.
  • Secrets, reusable credentials, protected session material and private personal data must not be published in transparency artifacts.
