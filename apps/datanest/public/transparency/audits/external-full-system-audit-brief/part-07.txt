# 24. Severity and Prioritization Rules

| Level | Use when | Example | Target response |

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| BLOCKER | Core workflow cannot proceed or release cannot be trusted. | Login impossible; deployment points to wrong DB; destructive data corruption. | Immediate stop / hotfix. |

| CRITICAL | Security, authorization, governance, data integrity, or severe production reliability risk. | Unauthorized vote; cross-project data leak; ledger overspend. | Urgent governed fix before expansion. |

| HIGH | Major user journey broken, misleading, or operationally expensive. | External AI result attaches to wrong Job; scheduler unsafe recovery. | Next release / highest backlog. |

| MEDIUM | Material friction, maintainability debt, incomplete observability, or non-critical defects. | Confusing status, duplicate screens, partial responsive breakage. | Planned optimization. |

| LOW | Polish, wording, minor consistency, low-frequency edge issue. | Spacing inconsistency, redundant label. | Bundle with nearby work. |

| OPPORTUNITY | New improvement not required to restore correctness. | Better export, simplified navigation, faster workflow. | Prioritize by impact/effort. |
