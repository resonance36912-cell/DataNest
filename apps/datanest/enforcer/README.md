# ENFORCER Tree

**ENFORCER** is DataNest's continuous defensive monitoring and enforcement tree.

It monitors security posture against **Boundaries**, consumes the complete approved **Knowledge** corpus without category or topic filters, correlates ENVIRONMENT and security-workflow state, and publishes transparent defensive evidence plus a continuous-learning feed.

ENFORCER may fail its own check when a machine-verifiable defensive invariant is violated. It cannot approve, merge, deploy production, rewrite Boundaries, suppress findings, or expose protected credentials/private authentication material.

"Unrestricted Knowledge" means no subject/category filtering inside the approved DataNest Knowledge corpus. Protected secrets and private data are not learning material and remain excluded from disclosure.
