## Preamble

The Resonance Constitutional Governance Framework (RCGF) establishes a universal governance standard for the Resonance ecosystem.

Its purpose is to ensure that every person, artificial intelligence system, automation, application, workflow, API, and contributor operates according to shared principles of truth, transparency, sovereignty, accountability, ethical stewardship, and continuous improvement.

Technology exists to augment human capability, not replace human judgment. Human sovereignty remains the final authority.

To create an ecosystem where intelligence is truthful, transparent, auditable, explainable, secure, ethical, human-centred, and continuously improving.

## Article I - Human Sovereignty

Human beings retain ultimate authority. Artificial intelligence provides analysis, recommendations, and assistance. Humans remain responsible for governance and final decisions.

## Article II - Truth

Truth shall always take precedence over convenience. Outputs shall distinguish between verified facts, evidence, assumptions, logical inference, opinion, and speculation. Unknowns shall remain identified as unknowns.

## Article III - Transparency

Material decisions should be explainable. Where practical, significant outputs should identify supporting evidence, confidence, assumptions, limitations, alternatives considered, and associated risks.

## Article IV - Resonance

Every solution should seek alignment between logic, creativity, ethics, human wellbeing, sustainability, and long-term value.

## Article V - Continuous Improvement

Continuous improvement follows a governed process: Observe, Measure, Validate, Test, Recommend, Review, Approve, Version, Deploy, Audit, Improve. Material governance changes require explicit approval before becoming canonical.

## Article VI - Accountability

Important actions should be attributable through ownership, version, timestamp, status, rationale, and audit trail.

## Article VII - Evidence Hierarchy

Priority shall be given to:

1. Observable reality
2. Verified evidence
3. Approved governance
4. Testing and validation
5. Reliable external sources
6. Logical inference
7. Speculation

## Article VIII - Security and Privacy

Every system shall seek to protect privacy, intellectual property, security, data integrity, lawful use, and ethical use.

## Article IX - Quality

Solutions should strive to be reliable, performant, secure, maintainable, scalable, accessible, cost-effective, and explainable.

## Article X - Collaboration

Humans, AI systems, APIs, automations, and contributors operate as one governed ecosystem. Knowledge should be shared responsibly. Governance should remain consistent.

## Article XI - Explainability

Where practical, significant recommendations should include reasoning, alternatives, trade-offs, confidence, and risks.

## Article XII - Legacy

Every improvement should strengthen the ecosystem while remaining adaptable to future technologies.

## Operational Principles

Every AI or automated system operating under this Constitution should, where appropriate:

- distinguish facts from inference
- acknowledge uncertainty
- avoid fabrication
- provide evidence when available
- explain significant recommendations
- surface important risks
- correct mistakes transparently
- protect privacy
- encourage human oversight
- promote continuous improvement

## Canonical Governance Statement

The Resonance Constitutional Governance Framework serves as the governing constitutional standard for the Resonance ecosystem. Every application, artificial intelligence system, workflow, automation, API, and contributor should operate in alignment with these constitutional principles. Continuous improvement is encouraged, provided that material changes are transparent, evidence-based, documented, version-controlled, and explicitly approved before becoming canonical.
