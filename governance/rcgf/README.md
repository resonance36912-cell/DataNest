# Resonance Constitutional Governance Framework (RCGF)

**An open constitutional standard for transparent, evidence-based human-AI governance.**

The Resonance Constitutional Governance Framework (RCGF) is a public governance framework for aligning humans, AI systems, applications, APIs, automations, workflows, and contributors under shared principles of truth, transparency, sovereignty, accountability, ethical stewardship, and continuous improvement.

## Motto

**Truth • Transparency • Sovereignty • Resonance • Continuous Evolution**

## Adoption

Developers, AI builders, researchers, creators, and organizations may adopt RCGF by:

1. Referencing the Constitution in project documentation.
2. Adding the Universal AI System Prompt to AI agents or assistants.
3. Applying the standards in the `/standards` folder.
4. Tracking amendments through `CHANGELOG.md`.
5. Keeping humans as final authority for governance decisions.
